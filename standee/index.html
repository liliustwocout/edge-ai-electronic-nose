Conventional Sensor Challenges Current Limitations
  • [1]
    Lethal Toxic Gases — H₂S & NH₃: Rapid olfactory fatigue at 10 ppm; severe fatality above 50 ppm — IDLH threshold. Induces pipeline corrosion and fire hazards at natural gas compressor stations.
  • [2]
    Static Thresholds & Delayed Alarms: Legacy isolated detectors trigger only after broad atmospheric dispersion, missing early micro-accumulation kinetics.
  • [3]
    Costly False Alarms: Cross-sensitivity to humidity, ambient thermal drift, and machinery smoke triggers abrupt line shutdowns, incurring severe operational losses.
Edge AI Electronic Nose Breakthrough Proposed Solution
  • [1]
    Hybrid Sensor Array & Custom Circuits: Pairs electrochemical ZE03-H₂S with custom dual semiconductor modules — custom MQ136 & MQ2 conditioning — to suppress circuit noise and thermal drift.
  • [2]
    Gas Kinetic Fingerprinting: Harnesses a 60-second wave cycle — WaveCycle 250 points — to extract distinct kinetic signatures for hazardous gases: H₂S, NH₃, Clean Air.
  • [3]
    Preemptive Forecasting +20s: Computes time-series derivatives ΔC/Δt to track dispersion velocity, issuing predictive warnings 20 seconds before reaching lethal thresholds.
